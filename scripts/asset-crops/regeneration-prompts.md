# Regeneration Prompts

These prompts are for regenerating raw source sheets for manual cropping. Keep the sheets as original source art. Do not rebuild them from already-cropped icons.

## Weapons

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: raw source sheet for RPG item icon cropping
Primary request: create one large clean fantasy RPG item atlas sheet focused on WEAPONS only, for later manual cropping into square icons.
Subject: rows of individual fantasy weapons including old iron sword, wolf fang blade, slime-coated sword, silver sickle blade, obsidian hammer, void blade, frost weapon, thorn dagger, bone knife, hunter spear, ritual blade, simple iron sword, rare heroic sword, legendary dragon-forged weapon.
Style/medium: polished dark fantasy game item concept art, painterly semi-realistic icons, consistent with a moody RPG UI, not cartoonish, no pixel art.
Composition/framing: every weapon is separated with generous padding, centered in its own invisible square cell, no overlapping, no frames, no labels, no text, no UI, neutral dark background.
Lighting/mood: dramatic but readable, soft rim light, high contrast silhouettes.
Color palette: dark steel, muted gold, cold blue, poison green, ember red accents; cohesive with existing dark fantasy game assets.
Constraints: no text, no watermark, no numbers, no decorative frames, no cropped-off items, keep all objects fully visible and easy to crop.
