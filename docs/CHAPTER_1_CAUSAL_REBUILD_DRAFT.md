## Chapter Thesis

The player investigates monsters and discovers that the monsters are also
fleeing from, reacting to, and exploiting the same abnormal pressure along the
road.

Core feeling: `我正在靠近真相，而真相也在凝視著我。`

## Chapter Rhythm

`survival -> open three-site observation -> hunted on the learned return route -> forced town repair -> deeper ecological route -> Boss convergence`

Do not turn this into the repeated template `three investigations -> route Boss
-> town report -> main Boss`. The three sites establish an observation rhythm;
the mantis changes that rhythm by using the player's own route.

## Opening Closure And New Problem

Previous condition:

- The protagonist accepts a guild investigation into the silent southern road.
- Monster concentration, north-leaning grass and black roots, and a towering
  shadow are visible but not yet understood.

Result:

- The protagonist is defeated by the shadow and reaches the isolated town by
  accident.
- The original investigation is unfinished, but the protagonist has personally
  confirmed that the town is cut off.

New practical problem:

- The only known way out has collapsed.
- The town needs to determine what happened to the road before anyone can leave,
  report to the guild, or safely restore contact.

Required performance checkpoint:

- Mia may describe the protagonist's symptoms and survival condition.
- The elder may frame which route risk the town can afford.
- Frey may establish how the southern road was used and who failed to return.
- Nobody may issue a detached order to `調查三個地點`.
- The next locations must be inferred from evidence already on the protagonist,
  the broken approach, and the last known route segments.

## Three-Segment Evidence Chain

The sites hold three different parts of the missing route. Each discovery needs
its own performed scene before its state, objective, or handbook output updates.

### Field Ridge: Direction Evidence

Observed:

- Animal tracks originally moved toward town.
- Near the black-root area they turn sharply sideways.
- Human boot prints continue toward the hunter walkway.
- There is no chase, struggle, or dragging trace.

Provisional interpretation:

- The missing people were not attacked here.
- The animals were not deliberately approaching town; they were avoiding a
  particular area.

Knowledge boundary:

- This establishes movement and avoidance, not the identity or cause of the
  pressure.

Output only after performance:

- Record the direction evidence.
- Open the reason to inspect the walkway used by the human tracks.

### Hunter Walkway: Learned-Behavior Evidence

Observed:

- Silver thread was originally a return or warning marker.
- One section was removed and retensioned at an effective ambush height.
- Its resin differs from ordinary mantis secretion.
- Repeated observation traces exist below the walkway where a predator could
  watch human movement.

Provisional interpretation:

- A creature has learned to use human roads and markers to hunt.
- It may explain the local attacks, but not yet the wider abnormality.

Teaching purpose:

- Monsters are not only combat targets. They receive environmental pressure,
  adapt, and change behavior.

Output only after performance:

- Record altered silver thread and learned ambush behavior.
- Preserve the player's traversal order so the later ambush can answer it.

### Old Camp: Underground Evidence

Observed:

- The campfire ash is cold and shows no recent fire.
- Black roots beneath the ash remain warm.
- Soot lies below the stones rather than above the fire bed.
- The roots contract or pulse northward.

Required prose boundary:

> 我撥開冷灰，指尖碰到的根卻是溫的。

Do not let narration announce that the pressure comes from underground. That is
still a larger hypothesis.

Contradiction:

- A clever predator could exploit roads and markers.
- It cannot explain directional warmth and movement in underground root systems.

Output only after performance:

- Record the warm-root contradiction.
- Do not silently synthesize all three sites in the objective UI.
- Eli may later place this beside the route and date records, but only within his
  archival knowledge boundary.

## Evidence Synthesis Checkpoint

The first two sites support a reasonable temporary answer: one intelligent
predator is operating along the road.

The old camp disproves that answer as a complete cause. The scene must leave the
player with a larger problem rather than an NPC's final explanation.

The synthesis performance must establish:

- Animals avoid one direction.
- Humans continued along the constructed route.
- A predator learned the route.
- Underground roots show a directional condition the predator cannot create.

After the performance, the active question becomes whether the predator has also
learned the protagonist's route.

## Silver Snare: Player Action Causes The Ambush

Trigger:

- The player has physically completed the three-site route.

Performance:

- On return, road markers have been turned.
- Silver thread has been restrung along the order of paths the player just used.
- The Ambush Mantis blocks the route the protagonist is most likely to choose.

Causal meaning:

- The mantis is not a scheduled Boss appearing after three counters reach one.
- The player's investigation taught it the player's movement pattern.
- Even if runtime geography uses a fixed encounter, the scene must show that the
  monster acted because of the player's route.

Immediate consequence:

- The player survives the learned ambush.
- Recovered silver thread remains physical evidence rather than generic repair
  material or a disconnected gathering objective.

## Forge Cross-Closure

The recovered silver thread becomes a temporary bellows linkage. The player does
not receive a separate task to gather forge-repair materials.

Visible consequence:

- The bellows operates again.
- The first repair queue contains a leaking pot, door hinges, and a cart wheel.
- The forge helps ordinary town life before it serves as a new-weapon dispenser.
- The market remains closed.

New practical capacity:

- Damaged equipment and tools can be handled well enough to support a deeper
  route into the forest.

Next question:

- Where do the black roots converge?
- Is the Forest Guardian creating the pressure, or enduring it?

## Forest Guardian Convergence

The deeper route follows the root pattern established by prior evidence. It is
not unlocked only because the mantis flag became true.

Boss aftermath performance:

- The Forest Guardian falls.
- The black roots do not disappear.
- Their pulse still points north.
- Nearby animals begin to disperse.
- Underground pressure remains.

Meaning available to the player:

- The guardian was a convergence point and reactive victim.
- It was not the ultimate source.

Do not let a character state the entire future cause. The environment proves
only that defeating this Boss did not remove the deeper pressure.

## Town Validation And Chapter Closure

The return is not a praise scene or reward summary. The town visibly tests
whether the player's actions mattered:

- Tavi relights one lamp that could not previously remain lit.
- The blacksmith repairs residents' daily objects.
- Mia confirms that the protagonist has substantially recovered while retaining
  a record of the abnormal symptoms.
- The regional map visibly opens the route into the next chapter.
- The market remains closed.

Chapter closure:

- Immediate road use returns.
- The true northward pressure remains unresolved.

Chapter 2 causal handoff:

- Because Chapter 1 made the road usable again, the road can now deliver an old
  empty box back into town.
- The Chapter 2 problem is therefore caused by the Chapter 1 victory, not opened
  as an unrelated market quest.
